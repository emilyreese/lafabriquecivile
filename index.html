WHO WE ARE

Who we are

La Fabrique Civile is a nonprofit association (loi 1901) founded by a small team with backgrounds in product, research, and civic work. We operate independently, without political affiliation, and reinvest all resources into our mission.

ADVISORY BOARD

Advisory board

We are supported by a small, informal advisory board: one person per domain (health, education, social issues, etc.) who help us think better and see further.

Advisors may:

  • surface relevant associations or initiatives,
  • contribute ideas or short pieces of content,
  • connect us with other practitioners or thinkers,
  • help identify meaningful public-interest projects.

The advisory board has no governance role and no administrative responsibilities.

FAQ

FAQ

What kind of organization is La Fabrique Civile?

A nonprofit, public-interest organization focused on research, experimentation, and practical civic tools.

Do you give grants or funding?

No. We build projects, platforms, and content, and we help surface and support existing initiatives.

Are your projects political?

No. La Fabrique Civile is non-partisan and independent.

Can anyone use your tools?

Yes. Our projects are designed to be free and accessible to the widest possible audience.

How are you funded?

Through donations, grants, and partnerships aligned with our mission.
